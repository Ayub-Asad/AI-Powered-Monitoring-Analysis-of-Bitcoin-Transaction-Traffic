## Verified defects fixed

- Seven generator tests used an obsolete root-level script path and silently skipped. Corrected path; tests were neither deleted nor weakened.
- Negative sub-satoshi fees rounded to accepted negative zero. Reject negative raw fees before rounding, including numeric underflow strings.
- Oversized epoch numbers and UTC datetime underflow/overflow raised uncaught OverflowError. They now produce row validation errors.
- V1 generation is intentionally retained; v2 replaces approximate anomaly budgeting and zero-budget forced clusters with exact budgets and complete scenarios.

## Dataset-quality checks and limitations

`data/v2/quality_report.json` contains numerical quantiles by label, per-label country/ASN/port/hour/date frequency tables, and descriptive single-threshold balanced accuracies. Highest scores by field: amount 0.615, fee 0.568, fee rate 0.542, inputs 0.628, outputs 0.617, UTC hour 0.542, weekday 0.527. These are same-dataset diagnostics, NOT trained or held-out model results. No anomalous-only contextual value with support at least five was found among checked fields.

This does not prove freedom from multivariate shortcuts. Injected chains/dust remain regular, capped chain fees can form distinctive combinations, and small address/observer pools affect behaviour. Country/ASN/IP are synthetic observations, not GeoIP or wallet ownership. Port/geo scenarios may not be detectable using default behavioural features. V2 is not a full UTXO ledger or representative real-world Bitcoin traffic. Exact legacy flows remain unavailable; whole-window wallet aggregates require careful temporal/group evaluation. Address checks remain structural; coinbase/addressless outputs and XML are unsupported.
